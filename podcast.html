Listen as a podcast

Every edition is also read aloud, in a gentle British voice. Add this feed to your podcast app and each evening's Long View arrives by itself, ready for a walk at dusk, the drive home or a quiet cup of tea.

{{ feed }}

Adding it to your app

  • Apple Podcasts (iPhone): open the Library tab, tap ⋯ at the top, choose Follow a Show by URL and paste the address.
  • Overcast: tap +, then Add URL, and paste it.
  • Pocket Casts: paste the address into the search box.
  • Other apps: look for “add by URL” or “add RSS feed”.

Your app keeps your place in each episode, and you can change the speed there too.
